How it works

Write. She calls. You go play.

No login. No camera. A telephone call with someone who wants you believing in the next shot.

01 · Choose

Half hour or 1 hour

Choose a length and leave your name, number, and what feels difficult right now. Payment is set up on the call, not on this site.

02 · The call

She telephones you

Not a Zoom link. A real phone call, nationwide or worldwide. Two voices. No screen in the way.

03 · The inner game

Notice, challenge, install

We find the thought that is capping you, challenge the old blueprint, and install a new one you can take to the next shot.

04 · The follow-up

Come back if it helped

Book another session when you are ready. Refer a golfer you know. If you want to leave a bonus, Danielle will share how on the phone.

What a call feels like

You answer. Danielle asks how the game has been, not how you scored. You talk about the thought that shows up, the round you cannot enjoy, or why the clubs are still in the garage.

You hang up with one clear next step. No homework packet. No video recap. A plan, and a person who will call.

Ready when you are.

A half hour or one hour. Payment is set up when Danielle calls.
