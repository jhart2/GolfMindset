Background

I saw him get back to the game. I wanted that for more golfers.

This is not a polished myth. It is a new practice that started with one person, a lot of motivation, and a simple idea: a phone call can get a golfer moving again.

In Danielle’s words

I tried motivation from every angle.

And now he is back chipping and putting and playing some golf. That is the whole origin of this practice. I figured I could motivate other golfers too — people who feel the same way he did.

Young golfers. Middle-aged golfers. Older golfers. The ones who hung the bag up, who got busy, who got discouraged, who think the game has left them. I want to talk to you.

Not on video. Not in a webinar. On the telephone, the old-school way. Two voices. A plan to get you back on the grass.

Why this work

Because motivation worked. Not as a slogan — as something that got a golfer chipping, putting, and playing again.

Who I call

Golfers of every age who miss the game and need a voice that will not let them stay on the couch.

How I show up

On the phone. Direct. Human. No camera, no performance. Just a conversation that points you back to the course.

The next details are easy

A headshot, your last name as you want it on the site, and the number golfers should call can drop in anytime. The goal will not change: get golfers back to the game.
